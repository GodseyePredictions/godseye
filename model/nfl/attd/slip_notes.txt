Model: a structural layer (team TDs × each player's share of the opportunities) plus gradient boosting, refit on every game through the most recent one. Out-of-sample 2025–26: log loss 0.387, AUC 0.76. On the 223 shipped ATD legs in your ledger it priced slightly better than the old engine (0.646 vs 0.652), but the gap isn't significant.
Personnel: the official injury report is applied (Out/Doubtful removed), plus data/personnel.csv overrides. Jacoby Brissett is removed from ARI (traded to BAL per your ledger; the nflverse roster lags). Re-run at inactives, about 90 minutes before each kickoff.
Singles only (kept off every ticket): Questionable players, and anyone who missed 2+ of his team's last 4 games.
Play-at cushion: WR +6 points, TE/QB +4, RB +3. WRs that made tickets ran about 7 points hot in the 2026 ledger (a selection effect), so they need the longest prices.
Long tickets compound small errors: an 8–12 leg ticket at a few percent or less is a lottery ticket even when every leg is fairly priced. Size by quarter-Kelly. The 4- and 6-leg are the tickets the model actually likes.
Weather: NWS kickoff-hour forecasts for every outdoor US game. International games have no NWS coverage, so weather there is treated as neutral.
Top-end calibration: in the 2026 walk-forward, players priced 60%+ scored 77% of the time (34 legs); in 2025–26 backtesting the same bucket ran about 4 points over. Treat 65%+ RBs as roughly fair.
