Probabilities come from the ATTD model (data only: play-by-play, snaps, injuries, Vegas lines, NWS weather; no media picks). ATD = chance of at least one rushing or receiving TD if he plays. Joint chances use the model's measured correlations: teammates' TDs are negatively correlated, opponents' slightly positive, and a QB's pass yards move with his receivers' yards. Cash-out values are estimates; bet365 sets its own offer.
